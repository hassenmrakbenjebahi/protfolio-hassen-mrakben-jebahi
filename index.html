My path into software started with mobile. During my engineering studies at ESPRIT in Tunis I built a recruitment app with AI-generated quizzes and native iOS and Android e-commerce apps — and kept running into the same truth: every good screen sits on a backend someone has to design well.

So I followed the stack down. At D-Talk, first as an intern and then part-time, I built REST APIs and the React Native frontend, integrated the Konnect payment API, and took ownership of CI/CD, Docker containers and Azure deployments. A year at Philipps University Marburg brought me to Germany.

Today I’m building tripInsel on my own. I like problems where several systems must agree — payments, roles, deployment, models — and I care that the result is automated and monitored. I’m looking for a role that combines backend engineering, DevOps ownership and practical AI integration.
